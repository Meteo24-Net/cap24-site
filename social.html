A Green Media Foundation demonstration

One CAP message, one visual language: from a social post to the full alert on the phone.

An alerting authority would publish the post on its own account. The post is drawn from the CAP fields of a live official alert, in the languages the authority sent. Tap the link, not the picture: platforms do not make pictures clickable.

Rules on this page
  • Text exactly as published, never translated or rewritten. Missing fields are shown as missing.
  • Colour: the authority's declared warning level, or CAP24 default colours from severity.
  • The header band pulses slowly and the arrow moves. Nothing flashes. Both stop if your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Every post carries "Demonstration, not an official publication".
  • MP4 export for upload to platforms PLANNED
