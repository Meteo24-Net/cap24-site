    What you are looking at

    Alerting authorities around the world already publish their warnings in CAP, the international standard for public alerts (OASIS CAP 1.2, ITU-T X.1303). CAP24 relays such alerts verbatim to broadcast, IPTV and phone screens, under the approval of each country's authorities and as part of its national warning system. This page shows the alerts as published. AI never creates, translates or changes alert content.

    Open any alert and choose View as TV overlay to see it fill a broadcast screen. Coming next: the same alert on a phone reached by QR code, and in a visual-only view for deaf viewers.

    Data. Alerts: Alert-Hub.org and the alerting authorities, through the public "CAP Alerts Feed" layer hosted by Esri. CAP alerts published by the Alert Hub are in the public domain. Only sources marked official in the Alert-Hub source catalogue (listed in the WMO Register of Alerting Authorities) are shown. Base map: OpenFreeMap, © OpenMapTiles, data © OpenStreetMap contributors. TV view outlines: Natural Earth. Map software: MapLibre GL JS.

    Who. CAP24 is developed by MTÜ Green Media Foundation (Estonia, registry 80661721), with Smartnet (Georgia) as technology provider and Infozavri Group LLC (Georgia) as delivery partner. Teimuraz Tsagareishvili founded Green Media Foundation and Smartnet; Infozavri is independently owned.
