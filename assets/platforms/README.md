Preparation: converted to PNG and reduced to 128 px. The LinkedIn and TikTok files arrived with a transparency
checkerboard printed into the picture; only that background was removed (TikTok: its black tile kept, corners rounded
as in the file). The marks themselves were not redrawn or recoloured.

Without a file, its button shows the platform name only.

Rules (spec GMF-WEB-SOC-001, section 8.10): the platforms' own icons, used unchanged; check each brand's rules
(Meta for Facebook and Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok) and record the source and the check above before the page is
announced. The page states: "Formats sized for these platforms. Not affiliated with or endorsed by them."
