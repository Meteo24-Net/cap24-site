Notify me about warnings for this area PROPOSED
Design intent only: nothing is stored or sent. You would choose an area, with no account and no GPS stored. A notification would carry the authority's headline as sent and open this page. It would be a companion service that may be delayed, and it does not replace official emergency alerts. Needs agreement from the alerting authority and a data-protection review.

One pre-recorded clip per CAP code (level or severity, hazard type, each responseType), never the free text. It is a summary of the codes, not the full message: the ticker carries the headline and instruction as sent. Clips are placeholders until recorded by Deaf signers and approved by the national Deaf association and the alerting authority.
